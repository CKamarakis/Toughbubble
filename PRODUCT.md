# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Everyday people and early-stage small businesses who find tools like Notion and Miro too heavy. They are not tech-focused. They use ToughBubble alone, to organise, plan and think things through. There is no collaboration yet.

## Product Purpose

One calm personal workspace for notes, Storms (light visual boards) and, later, Tasks (a light Kanban board). It should be easy, affordable and uncomplicated without losing sophistication; usability comes first. Free for now: anyone can sign up, with email confirmation.

## Positioning

Provisional, refined as the product grows: the sophistication of Notion and Miro with none of the setup; flexible without becoming annoying or confusing.

## Operating Context

- Desktop browser first. Mobile apps, a marketing site and an MCP connector are possible later.
- One tree of projects, folders, notes and Storms that the user nests and orders freely.
- Product decisions and open questions live in `docs/project-context.md`; the build plan and visual tokens in `openspec/config.yaml`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Built incrementally: build, test, move forward. Many product facts are deliberately undecided and are defined as the work goes.
- Watch database size and usage, security, scalability and permissions.
- Images cost storage, so they are optimised for the web, at least on the free tier.
- Undecided: the scope of Tasks, and whether it becomes a kind of item in the tree.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: ToughBubble. The disc logo and the light-weight wordmark are specified in `openspec/specs/app-branding/spec.md`.
- Voice: warm and playful, but not cliché and not American-style hype.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet: no users, testers, quotes or waitlist. Never invent testimonials, user numbers or other proof; the user will say when real evidence exists.

## Product Principles

1. Made for ordinary people, not tech enthusiasts or teams chasing the next tool.
2. Flexible without being confusing: every option earns its place.
3. Light by default; sophistication sits underneath, not on the surface.
4. Affordable to run: keep storage and usage lean.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1) for body text, in both light and dark themes.
