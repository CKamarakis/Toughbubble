Kept by `/kit:roadmap`; checked with `node <kit>/scripts/roadmap.mjs check`. Prose between the headings is yours; the lists under Order, Now and Waiting on you have a fixed shape.

## North star

One calm personal workspace (notes, Storms and later Tasks) for everyday people and small businesses who find Notion and Miro too heavy. Sophisticated without the setup; usability comes first.

## Principles

- **Usability first:** if a feature makes the app confusing, it waits.
- **Calm and uncomplicated:** flexible without becoming annoying.
- **Build in slices:** each one usable on its own; build, test, move forward.
- **Solo first:** no collaboration until the personal workspace is solid.
- **Watch the costs:** database size, security and permissions.
- **Desktop browser first:** mobile and tablet come after.
