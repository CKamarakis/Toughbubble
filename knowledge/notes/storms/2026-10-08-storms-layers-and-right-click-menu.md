Feature 3 of the storms requirements. Screenshots: `knowledge/assets/Storms/sticky notes basics/` (right-click menu, layers, covered-note selection).

## Layer order

- A new item goes on top of everything.
- Arrange: bring forward, bring to front, send backward, send to back. Shortcuts as in Miro: PgUp = front, PgDn = back, Shift+PgUp/PgDn = one step.
- No "Move to layer".
- Clicking the visible part of a partly covered item selects it. Where items overlap, the top one is selected. No Alt+click cycling to items underneath.
- Items dropped onto a shape just sit on top; they aren't contained and still move freely. Grouping is how items move together.

## Right-click menu (v1)

- Copy, paste, duplicate, delete.
- Arrange (submenu).
- Lock / unlock.
- Group / ungroup.
- Copy style / paste style: copy one item's look (fill, border, text formatting) and apply it to other items. Try it out and keep it if it feels useful.

- **Frames**: good to have, not now.
