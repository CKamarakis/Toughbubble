## How it works, roughly
Your theme choice is saved in this browser only, not in your account, so another device keeps its own. On touch screens the whole interface is drawn about 14% larger (8/7), while note text keeps the size set in editor settings.

## Why

- **Comfortable sizes on touch screens**: One rule enlarges the whole interface, so no screen gets missed. Fixing each component one by one would have meant dozens of edits that new screens could miss. It goes by the pointer type, not the screen width, so narrow desktop windows stay as they are and tablets get the larger sizes too. Laptops with a touch screen as their main pointer also get the larger sizes. The 8/7 factor makes regular text exactly 16px.
